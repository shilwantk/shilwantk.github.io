About me

I am an electronics engineer who stumbled across iOS app development as a fun side project and decided to make it my profession 5 years ago. Everything I have learned until now is self-taught through books, online courses, blogs, and of course, by writing bad code.

I’m currently a Senior Mobile Engineer at Music Tribe, contributing to creating a mobile interface for the HD-96 mixing console. I take pride in knowing when to use for-in and forEach in a code.

In my free time, you will find me reading contemporary fiction, trying new recipes, and working on my hybrid mobile application skills using Flutter.
